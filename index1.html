Maison Noir & Crimson

Founded in Paris and crafted in Florence, Noir & Crimson bridges architectural minimalism with baroque decadence. Each bag undergoes 48 hours of hand-finishing in our Florentine workshop, using only the finest leathers and gold-smithing techniques.

The signature palette of burgundy, black, white and gold reflects our devotion to timeless elegance. Every piece is serialized and comes with a lifetime warranty of craftsmanship.
